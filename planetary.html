Planetary Stability Thresholds

Planetary stability depends on a small number of critical thresholds. When crossed, systems shift — often abruptly and irreversibly.

Water sits at the center of climate variability, ecosystems, food systems, coastlines, and human security.

Global Water Fund monitors where water stress pushes systems toward instability — and how intervention preserves resilience.
