Planetary Stability Thresholds

Water and Planetary Stability

Planetary stability depends on a small number of critical thresholds. When crossed, systems shift — often abruptly and irreversibly.

Water sits at the center of several thresholds, linking climate variability, ecosystems, food systems, coastlines, and human security.

Global Water Fund focuses on where water stress pushes systems toward instability — and how strategic intervention preserves resilience.
