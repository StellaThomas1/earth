Planetary Stability Thresholds
Water and Planetary Stability
Planetary stability depends on a small number of critical thresholds. When crossed, systems shift — often abruptly and irreversibly.
Water sits at the center of many thresholds, linking climate variability, ecosystems, food systems, coasts, and human security. Global Water Fund focuses on where water stress pushes systems toward instability — and where intervention preserves resilience.
This is a strategic lens: engineered, decision-ready, and designed for action — not academic display.
