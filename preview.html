Water and Planetary Stability

Planetary stability depends on a small number of critical thresholds. When crossed, systems shift — often abruptly and irreversibly.

Water plays a central role in many of these thresholds, linking climate, ecosystems, food systems, and human security.

Global Water Fund focuses on understanding where water-related stress pushes systems toward instability — and how strategic intervention can preserve resilience.
